# SQB Bot - Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SQB Bot handles information when it is used on Discord servers.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, SQB Bot may process information such as:

* Discord user ID
* Discord server ID
* Discord username or nickname
* Discord role and permission information
* Information related to Squadron Battles
* Information provided through commands or interactions with the bot

The bot only processes information that is necessary to provide its features.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

* Provide SQB Bot features
* Manage Squadron Battles-related functions
* Identify users and Discord servers
* Store and display relevant SQB-related information
* Maintain and improve the bot

## 4. Data Storage

Information may be stored on the infrastructure used to operate SQB Bot.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## 5. Data Sharing

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the bot or comply with applicable law.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the bot's features or maintain required records.

## 7. Data Deletion

Users or server administrators may request deletion of information associated with SQB Bot where applicable.

Deletion requests can be made through the Discord server where the bot is used.

## 8. Discord

SQB Bot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SQB Bot's functionality or data processing practices change.

## 10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or SQB Bot's handling of information, please contact the bot's owner through the Discord server where the bot is used.
